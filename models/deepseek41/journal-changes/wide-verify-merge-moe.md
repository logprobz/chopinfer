# Wide direct MoE over the merged wide verify branch

The merge of the [wide direct MoE](moe-direct-wide.md) into the wide
verify branch (dense folds with their tuned schedules, HC fusion and
queries) changes the two allocation fixtures with the `wide_verify`
option (the C16 odd-width fixture at 65 rows and the C32 maximum fixture
at 192 rows). This is its proof against the first parent, the wide verify
branch.

The [layout declaration](wide-verify-merge-moe.json) is the MoE change's
declaration with its operation edits regenerated by
`tools/journal_proof/specs/operation_edits.py` from captures of the first
parent and of the merge. It adds the locked module
`bandwidth_moe_deepseek41_wide_sm120` wherever
`dense_decode_wide_deepseek41_sm120_v1` loads, with its 12 resolves on each
rank, and lists 2932 exact operation edits in each of the two journals:
1280 removed and 1600 added launches and 52 added releases. Per symbol and
side these are the counts of the MoE change's own declaration on its
parent; only their positions differ. Each journal grows by 420 lines: C16
odd-width from 190918 to 191338 and C32 maximum from 343656 to 344076.
Every other journal, create range and device history is identical.
